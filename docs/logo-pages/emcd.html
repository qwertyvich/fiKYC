Which cryptocurrencies can I mine with EMCD Pool?
+
EMCD supports mining for major cryptocurrencies , including Bitcoin (BTC), Litecoin (LTC), Dogecoin (DOGE), Bitcoin Cash (BCH), Ethereum Classic (ETC), Kaspa (KAS), and Dash
What payout system does EMCD use, and when are rewards distributed?
+
EMCD supports FPPS, PPS+, and PPLNS payout systems. FPPS and PPS+ include transaction fees in reward calculations, while PPLNS rewards miners based on their share contribution when a block is found. Rewards are credited daily and paid automatically
What is merged mining and how does it increase profitability?
+
Merged mining allows you to mine additional coins simultaneously without extra electricity consumption or losing your main hashrate. For example, you can earn DOGE together with LTC or FB together with BTC
How do I connect my ASIC or GPU miners to EMCD Pool?
+
Choose the coin you want to mine, select the nearest Stratum server, and enter your EMCD account name as the worker ID in your mining device settings
What are the pool fees?
+
Our pool fees range from 1.5% to 4% depending on the coin. There are no hidden fees or extra charges for daily payouts to your EMCD account
Why choose EMCD Mining Pool over other pools?
+
EMCD offers 99.9% pool uptime, global low-latency Stratum servers, merged mining opportunities, flexible payout systems including FPPS, PPLNS, and PPS+, transparent reward calculations, and 24/7 technical support
