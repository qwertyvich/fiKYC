Frequently Asked Questions

What is Walt and how is it different from other crypto apps?

Walt is a Telegram-native, all-in-one crypto app used by tens of millions of people worldwide. Formerly known as Wallet in Telegram, Walt brings crypto trading, investing, and everyday transactions together in one place.
With Walt, you can deposit, hold, and withdraw crypto; trade 200+ popular crypto assets; access real-world assets such as tokenized stocks, ETFs, and metals; and trade perpetual futures – all directly within Telegram.

What’s the difference between custodial and self-custodial services?

In a custodial service, we protect your crypto and manage the keys for you. If you lose access, we can help you recover your account. You don’t need to worry about seed phrases or backups. This is the standard setting for Walt.

Walt also gives you access to DeFi Account, which is self-custodial. This means you are fully in charge of your crypto. You hold your own private keys (called a seed phrase) and are responsible for keeping them safe. No one else can access your wallet, but if you lose your seed phrase, there’s no way to recover it.

Where do I find Walt app?

Walt is available directly in Telegram. Search for @walt and make sure the account has Telegram’s verification badge, or use the direct link.
For faster access, you can add Walt to your home screen or pin the @walt chat in Telegram.

What are the fees and how are transactions charged?

Walt users can send crypto to other Telegram users for free. For on-chain transactions to external wallets, standard blockchain network fees apply along with a transparent and flexible Wallet service fee. For example, sending any amount of USDT to an external address costs only 1 USDT when using the TON network. Additional fees may apply for swaps or fiat conversions.
For detailed information about current fee rates and limits, visit Fee rates and limits.

Is Walt in Telegram safe?

Walt is a custodial service that securely manages your private keys. To enhance security, we recommend enabling two-step verification in Telegram, setting a unique passcode for Walt, and managing active Telegram sessions to prevent unauthorized access. You can also enable biometric authentication as an added layer of protection.

For detailed steps and best practices, see How to secure your account.
